Research · Zenodo, 2026

Measuring the failures nobody sees

AI Model Performance in CV Generation and Consistency Testing: A Practical Six-Dimension Evaluation Framework

I asked four models to turn degree transcripts into a CV. The most dangerous failures were invisible: sections quietly left out, contact details invented, and no warning to the reader. The paper scores output on six dimensions instead of accuracy alone.

  • Completeness, contextual utilisation, factual accuracy, confidence calibration, consistency and specification adherence, each scored 1 to 5.
  • The "MISSING" instruction kept factual accuracy at 5/5 for all four models.
  • Asking to "fill in ALL sections" dropped DeepSeek's factual accuracy from 5 to 1.
